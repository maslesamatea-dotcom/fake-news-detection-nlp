# Data

The data are not included in the repository. Download them from Kaggle:

**Fake and Real News Dataset**
https://www.kaggle.com/clmentbisaillon/fake-and-real-news-dataset

Two files are needed: `Fake.csv` (23,481 articles) and `True.csv` (21,417 articles). Put them in this folder (`data/`).
Check the terms of use of the dataset on the Kaggle page.

The NLTK resources `stopwords` and `wordnet` are also required (downloaded with `nltk.download(...)`).
